JUMP TENNIS

First to 10 points wins. The ball may bounce once - if it touches the floor a second time (before someone hits it), the other player scores!

Player 1   A / D move   W jump (press again in the air to jump again!)   S dive down   Space = your ability
Player 2   ← / → move   ↑ jump (press again in the air to jump again!)   ↓ dive down   / = your ability
Every point you both get a NEW random ability (50 different ones, one is super rare!). Every point you both get a NEW random ability (50 different ones, one is super rare!). Touch the ball with your racket to send it to the other side. Esc = menu.
