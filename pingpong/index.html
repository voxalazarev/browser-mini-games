PING PONG

First to 11 points wins - but you must win by 2!

Player 1   W / S to move your paddle up and down
Player 2   ↑ / ↓ to move your paddle up and down
Real table-tennis rules: let the ball bounce once on your side before you return it - hit it before the bounce, or let it bounce twice, and it's a fault. Esc = menu.
