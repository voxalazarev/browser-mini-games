ROOFTOP PARKOUR

First-person-style split-screen city parkour! Run, jump, mantle and stick to walls (jump into a wall, then W/S climb and A/D move sideways, jump to leap off) up onto rooftops. Shoot a web at the other player: if they do not dodge it (swing key) in time, they become the tagger. Web has a 5s cooldown.

Player 1 (top view): A/D turn, W forward, S back, Space to jump/mantle, E web-swing (also dodges a web), Q shoot web
Player 2 (bottom view): ←/→ turn, ↑ forward, ↓ back, / to jump/mantle, . web-swing (also dodges a web), , shoot web
